Meet the Owners

Serving Leon, Iloilo Since 2022

Welcome to Zaki and Co. Laundry Hub! Established in 2022, our business is family-owned and operated with deep pride right here in Talacuan, Leon.

From day one, our mission has been simple: treat every client's clothes with the same care and attention we give our own. As a family business, customer satisfaction is personal to us. Whether you bring in your weekly family laundry, heavy comforters, or items for pressing, we are dedicated to providing clean, fresh results with a warm local touch.
